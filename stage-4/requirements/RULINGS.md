# Stage 4 — Rulings (Analyst)

with the rest of the specification. The stage-3 (RUL-S3-xx), stage-2 (RUL-S2-xx) and stage-1 (RUL-xx) tables follow unchanged
and still apply. "Tested" names the acceptance test that pins the ruling; "—" means guidance only.
